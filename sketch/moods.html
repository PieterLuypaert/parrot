Sketch for a Herdr mod · plan 9

parrot

A parrot that watches while you work. When it spots an error, in your terminal or beyond it, it startles. Click it and your own Claude works out what went wrong, with the fix as one button. You can also just ask it something.

How it goes

A mock Herdr window with three flows. It plays by itself; click the parrot, the buttons in the speech bubble or the question bar to join in. The Parrot button on the right of the tab bar turns it on or off. It needs a terminal pane in the tab: in a tab with only an agent session it cannot be turned on, and beside one it watches the terminal while you type to the agent.
